Google Calendar Timezone Reorder allows you to change the display order of timezones in the calendar sidebar without changing the primary timezone for events.

🎯 FEATURES:
• Drag and drop timezones to change display order
• Settings saved between sessions
• Automatically applied on page load
• Does not affect the primary timezone for events

💼 PERFECT FOR:
• Teams working across different timezones
• Frequent travelers
• International projects
• Coordinating meetings across timezones

📖 HOW TO USE:
1. Open Google Calendar with timezones configured (Settings → Time zone)
2. Click the extension icon in Chrome toolbar
3. Drag timezones to desired order (first in list will be on the left)
4. Click "Save" and refresh the calendar page

🔒 PRIVACY:
The extension does not collect or transmit any personal data. All settings are stored locally in your browser.

⚙️ REQUIREMENTS:
• Google Calendar must be open in browser
• At least 2 timezones must be configured in calendar settings
